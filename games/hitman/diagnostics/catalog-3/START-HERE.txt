HITMAN CO-OP / CATALOG-3 MOVEMENT TEST
OPS 0.1.19 / native preview.4.catalog.3 / HITMAN 3.290.1

1. Close HITMAN on both PCs. In the OPS HUB launcher, use Check for updates
   and Update & Restart. Both PCs should show 0.1.19.
2. Launch Freeform Training on both PCs. Wait 15 seconds, use Restart Mission
   once on each PC, then wait 15 seconds again. Host, join, ready and start.
3. For walking, face the yacht from spawn. Hold Left Ctrl for Instinct and
   follow the Ronnie Aguilar marker on both screens. Ronnie is Kalvin Ritter's
   bodyguard. Compare where Ronnie turns and stops while he walks. You can
   watch from the dock. If he stays put or the outline is out of range, report
   what you saw and keep both test NPCs alive and dressed.
4. For turning, have the host approach Terry by the car. Compare how smoothly
   Terry follows the host's movement, then check his hand and phone again.
5. Quit normally and save Retail\mods\HitmanCoop.log from both PCs.

Each run uses the host's simulation for Terry and Ronnie. Quit HITMAN before
starting another run. RESTORE-0.1.3.cmd restores the tested movement build.
