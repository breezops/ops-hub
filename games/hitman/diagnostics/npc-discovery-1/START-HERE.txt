HITMAN CO-OP / NPC DISCOVERY TEST
OPS 0.1.27 / HITMAN 3.290.1

1. Close HITMAN on both PCs. Use Check for updates and Update & Restart
   in the OPS HUB launcher. Confirm 0.1.27 on both PCs.
2. Launch HITMAN, wait at the main menu, then host and join. The host
   starts Freeform Training through HITMAN's menu.
3. Walk around for about 15 seconds once you can see each other. Leave
   NPCs undisturbed for this test so the two scans are easy to compare.
4. The host selects Restart Mission. Once you can see each other again,
   walk around for another 15 seconds.
5. Quit normally. Keep and share both Retail\mods\HitmanCoop.log files.

This build records which NPCs each PC can identify across the whole
mission, before and after restart. NPC behavior still runs locally.
