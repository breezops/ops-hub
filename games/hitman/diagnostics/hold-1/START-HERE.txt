HITMAN CO-OP / PRIVATE ONE-GUARD HOLD TEST / HOLD-1
OPS 0.1.10 / native preview.4.hold.1 / HITMAN 3.290.1

SOLO ONLY. Friends do not need to update. No host/join/Ready buttons.
This build can change one guard's native AI hold state when you press F8.
It is not shared NPC gameplay or a whole-game pause test.

STARTUP CHECK FIRST:
1. Close HITMAN and the old launcher. Open this folder's launcher.
2. Click INSTALL / REPAIR, then LAUNCH HITMAN.
3. Load Freeform Training alone, wait 15 seconds and reply "in mission".
4. Leave the game running. Do not press F8 until the developer checks that the
   job, action and hold-arbitration hooks all attached.

AFTER THE DEVELOPER CONFIRMS ATTACHMENT:
5. Use HITMAN's Restart Mission once. This supplies a fresh actor generation.
6. Approach a conscious walking guard within roughly 8 metres. Choose a guard
   who is the closest guard to you so the selection is unambiguous.
7. Press F8 ONCE while HITMAN has focus. Watch the closest guard: the test
   attempts to hold him for 8 seconds, then resume him automatically. Other
   NPCs and your character should keep working normally.
8. Stay nearby and watch for another 15 seconds, then quit HITMAN normally.
9. Tell the developer whether the guard stopped, resumed walking, and whether
   anything else looked wrong. The developer can read the log directly.

Do not knock anyone out, change disguise, attack or restart during the cycle.
If no guard is selected, the log explains why; do not keep repeating F8 blindly.
If the guard remains stuck, quit normally and report it. Only one selected
actor/cycle is allowed per process. A second test requires a full game restart.

The test refuses actors already held by the game and watches the verified native
hold setter for game/script interference. It does not prove all native hold
sources, cross-thread ordering or physical actor retirement are integrated.
No screen capture, game files or saves are included.

Close HITMAN and run RESTORE-0.1.3.cmd to return to tested multiplayer.
The bundled original rollback ZIP is checksum verified. Do not use SDK hot
reload. CHECK FOR UPDATES does not fetch this private build.
